## Portfolio-wide release gates

1. **Identity and scope:** named project, purpose, owner, environment, allowed data, and explicit exclusions.
2. **Evidence:** source, timestamp, verification method, confidence, limitations and provenance.
3. **Data protection:** minimization, access control, retention, deletion, and sensitive-data exclusions.
4. **Safety and quality:** threat model, misuse cases, test plan, acceptance criteria and independent review.
5. **Operations:** observability, incident response, backup/recovery and rollback evidence.
6. **Authorization:** explicit approval scoped to the exact change and target environment.

A general request to continue work does not grant production, publishing, financial, account-sync, or sensitive-data permissions.

## Portfolio status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED:** supported by reproducible evidence for the stated scope.
- **DOCUMENTED:** design or code exists, but behavior is not fully validated.
- **PROPOSED:** candidate design or next step.
- **PENDING:** blocked on evidence, review, owner, or authorization.
- **BLOCKED:** a known gate prevents progression.
- **UNKNOWN:** insufficient information; never treat as zero or complete.
